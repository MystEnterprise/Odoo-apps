Two Pipelines. One Module.

Most businesses manage customer leads AND internal innovation projects — but Odoo forces you to use separate tools. Success Pipeline runs both in parallel.

🌎 Leads Pipeline

Track customer sourcing requests from initial brief through supplier sourcing, margin approval, and deal close. Ball-in-court indicators show exactly who owns each deal at every stage.

⚡ Game-Changer Pipeline

Manage product innovation from Ideation through Feasibility, Sourcing, Prototype, Scale, Launch, and Market Validation. Create Odoo products directly from the Launch stage.

📈 Live KPI Dashboard

10 KPIs tracked automatically across both pipelines. Progress bars show actual vs target. Filter by period and product category. Pipeline simulator lets you model scenarios.

✅ Team Accountability

Non-negotiables tracker shows weekly commitments for each team member — visible to everyone. Company standards and personal commitments in separate columns.

🛒 Supplier Intelligence

Link suppliers directly to leads. Track Option A and B with full supplier details, MOQ, pricing, and lead times. Build institutional sourcing memory over time.

💌 Email & Comms Tracking

Automatic email alerts when briefs are sent or confirmed. Ball-in-court tracking logged in chatter. WhatsApp/WeChat log field to capture external communications.
